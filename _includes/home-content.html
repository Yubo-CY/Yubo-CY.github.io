I'm a master's student in computer science at Brown University. My research is on foundation models for physiological signals, mostly EEG and overnight sleep recordings: how to adapt them to new modalities and settings without labels, and how to tell whether they really generalize to new patients, hospitals and cohorts.

I'm currently a visiting student researcher with Prof. Yu Zhang in the SPNI Lab at Stanford School of Medicine, and a research intern with Prof. Haoqi Sun and Prof. M. Publications

ZeroMAG: Zero-Shot Multimodal Adapter Generation for Plug-and-Play EEG Foundation Models

Yubo Wang, Jingying Ma, Xinliang Zhou, Yangxuan Zhou, Jiquan Wang, Sha Zhao, Yiyuan Yang, Yi Ding, Ziyu Jia, Chenyu Liu, Cuntai Guan

Preprint, arXiv:2610.03546, 2026 arXiv PDF

Research

ZeroMAG

Mar 2026 – Present

Stanford SPNI Lab and Nanyang Technological University, with Prof. Yu Zhang and Prof. Cuntai Guan

A weight VAE and a conditional diffusion transformer generate adapter weights for a frozen EEG backbone from unlabeled target recordings. Across 3 backbones and 6 held-out datasets, it improves balanced accuracy by 7.22 points over EEG-only and comes within 0.5 points of supervised multimodal adaptation.

External blind tests for EEG depression models

May 2026 – Present

Stanford SPNI Lab, with Prof. Yu Zhang

Measures how much locally strong EEG models degrade on an independent clinical cohort, and corrects for site shift with domain-adversarial learning and prediction-bias correction that use only unlabeled local data.

PhysioRuler

Aug 2026 – Present

Stanford Medicine and Harvard Medical School / BIDMC, with Prof. Haoqi Sun and Prof. M. Brandon Westover

An interpretable representation of a whole night of polysomnography that goes beyond sleep staging, with interpretable axes for stage stability, slow-wave and sigma activity, and cardiorespiratory instability across EEG, EOG, ECG and breathing.

Tabula 2.0

Sep 2026 – Present

With Dr. Jiayuan Ding (Stanford University) and Dr. Qingsong Wen (University of Oxford)

Scaling the Tabula single-cell foundation model to more data and more modalities, toward a foundation model for heterogeneous scientific tables, including predicting how cells respond to perturbations.

Shortcut learning in EEG emotion recognition

Nov 2024 – Jun 2025

Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, with Prof. Ziyu Jia

Found that emotion-recognition models were exploiting cross-channel artifacts instead of physiological patterns, and built a variational Bayesian graph network that aligns channel relations across subjects (73.5% accuracy, leave-one-subject-out).
