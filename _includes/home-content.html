Research

ZeroMAG

Mar 2026 to now

Stanford SPNI Lab and Nanyang Technological University, with Prof. Yu Zhang and Prof. Cuntai Guan

Gives a frozen EEG foundation model access to companion physiological signals by generating plug-and-play adapter weights, with a structure-aware weight VAE, a conditional diffusion transformer and a mixture-of-experts decoder. It adapts to unseen subjects, datasets and modality settings with no target labels and no target-side training: +7.22 pp balanced accuracy over EEG-only across 3 backbones and 6 held-out datasets, within 0.50 pp of supervised multimodal adaptation.

Blind-testing EEG models across clinical cohorts

May 2026 to now

Stanford SPNI Lab, with Prof. Yu Zhang

Asks whether EEG models for depression diagnosis survive the move to an independent clinical cohort. Domain-adversarial learning and prediction-bias correction use only unlabeled local data, never the external test labels, and strict subject-level evaluation shows how much locally strong models degrade under site shift.

PhysioRuler

Aug 2026 to now

Stanford Medicine and Harvard Medical School / BIDMC, with Prof. Haoqi Sun and Prof. M. Brandon Westover

A reference-based representation of whole-night polysomnography, organized by sleep stage, stability, physiological system and time scale rather than staging accuracy alone. System-specific encoders for EEG, EOG, ECG and respiration, a hidden semi-Markov model of stage dynamics and CAP-informed weak labels give interpretable axes such as slow-wave and sigma activity and cardiorespiratory instability.

Tabula 2.0

Sep 2026 to now

With Dr. Jiayuan Ding (Stanford University) and Dr. Qingsong Wen (University of Oxford)

Extending the Tabula single-cell foundation model with multimodal inputs and larger-scale pretraining, toward a foundation model for unordered, heterogeneous scientific tables, including perturbation-conditioned prediction of cell state.

Shortcut learning in EEG emotion recognition

Nov 2024 to Jun 2025

Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, with Prof. Ziyu Jia

Showed that emotion-recognition models were exploiting cross-channel artifacts rather than physiological patterns, then built a variational Bayesian heterogeneous GNN that aligns inter-channel relations across subjects (73.5% accuracy, 71.85% F1, leave-one-subject-out).
